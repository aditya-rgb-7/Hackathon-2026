How MountainWatch Works

1

ESP32 SENSING

Capacitive sub-surface moisture probes & tipping-bucket rainfall sensors gather real-time hydraulic soil conditions.

2

TRANSMISSION

Encrypted lightweight JSON packets dispatched over cellular/Wi-Fi to the centralized monitoring ingestion engine.

3

ANALYSIS ENGINE

Statistical rate-of-change algorithms evaluate slope saturation velocity and flag threshold breaches.

4

DISASTER WARNING

Automated warning distribution to local community checkpoints, safe shelter routing, and SDRF response teams.
